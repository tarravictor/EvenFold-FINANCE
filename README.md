# EvenFold FINANCE

### Your money. Organized.

EvenFold FINANCE is a simple personal finance tracker designed to help you understand where your money goes, keep track of what others owe you, split expenses with friends, and work toward your financial goals.

---

## Access EvenFold FINANCE

### [Open EvenFold FINANCE →](https://tarravictor.github.io/EvenFold-FINANCE/)

Click the link above to open the website and sign in to your account.

---

## What Can You Do?

### Track Your Expenses

Record your everyday spending and see how much you've spent throughout the week.

Add expenses such as:

- Food and drinks
- Transportation
- School expenses
- Shopping
- Subscriptions
- Bills
- Other personal expenses

Your records help you understand where your money is going and how much you have spent.

---

### Track Money You've Lent

Lent money to a friend or classmate?

EvenFold FINANCE helps you keep track of your **loans or "utang"** so you don't have to remember everything yourself.

You can record:

- Who borrowed the money
- How much they borrowed
- What the money was for
- When you lent it
- Whether it has been paid

---

### Split Group Expenses

Going out with friends or paying for something as a group?

Use the **Group Bill Splitter** to divide shared expenses and keep track of how much each person needs to pay.

Perfect for:

- Group meals
- School projects
- Trips
- Shared purchases
- Other group expenses

---

### Set Financial Goals

Have something you're saving for?

Create financial goals and track your progress over time.

For example:

- Emergency fund
- New phone or laptop
- School expenses
- Travel
- Personal purchases
- General savings

---

### View Your Financial Records

Your dashboard gives you an organized view of your financial activity so you can quickly understand your spending, loans, balances, and goals.

---

### Export Your Records

Need a copy of your financial information?

EvenFold FINANCE provides export options for supported financial records, making it easier to keep a personal backup or review your finances elsewhere.

---

## Getting Started

Using EvenFold FINANCE is simple:

1. **Open the website** using the link above.
2. **Create your account** or sign in.
3. **Add your first expense.**
4. Record any money you've lent to other people.
5. Create group bills when splitting expenses.
6. Set financial goals you want to reach.
7. Check your dashboard regularly to monitor your finances.

---

## Your Account

Each user has their own EvenFold FINANCE account.

Your financial records are connected to your account, so make sure you use your own login and keep your password private.

Always sign out when using a shared or public computer.

Your financial information is personal.

EvenFold FINANCE is designed so account information and financial records are associated with the appropriate signed-in user.
